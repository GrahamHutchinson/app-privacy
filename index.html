Privacy Policy – IsoBox

Last updated: 17 October 2026

IsoBox ("the app") is made by Graham Hutchinson. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: there is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and your music, recordings and settings stay on your device.

What stays on your device

Your music files, playlists, favourites, tags, lyrics, chords, separated parts (stems), voice or instrument recordings, equaliser and practice settings, and the results of the library analysis are stored on your device. I do not receive them and cannot see them. If you choose an export folder, files you export are saved there, in a place you control (for example iCloud Drive, which is managed by Apple under its own privacy policy).

Permissions the app asks for

  • Microphone: only when you record along with a song. Recordings stay on your device.
  • Camera: only to scan printed lyrics or capture an album cover. Pictures are processed on your device and are not saved to your photo library or uploaded.
  • Photos and Files: only the pictures or files you choose.
  • Apple Music library: only to copy songs you choose into the app.
  • Local network: only while the Send / Receive Music screen is open, to find and transfer music with another nearby device running the app.

Online lookups

When you open a screen that needs them, the app contacts these services. Only the text needed for the lookup is sent, along with your device's IP address (which any internet connection reveals) and the app's name and version.

  • Wikipedia / Wikimedia: artist name, to show a short biography and photograph.
  • MusicBrainz and the Cover Art Archive: artist and album name, to search for album artwork.
  • LRCLIB (only in versions where Find Lyrics Online is available): song title and artist, to search for lyrics.

These services have their own privacy policies. I do not link these requests to you and I do not receive them.

Links that open your web browser

The lyrics editor has "Search the Web" and "Search LRCLIB" buttons. They open your own web browser at a search page for the song's title and artist. The search happens in your browser, outside the app, under that website's own privacy policy. The app does not receive anything from it and does not download lyrics for you.

Machine learning

Separating a song into parts, listening for chords, measuring each song's loudness to even out the volume, and analysing your library are done entirely on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

Tracking and sharing

The app does not track you, does not contain third-party advertising or analytics code, and does not sell or share personal information.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

TestFlight

If you install a test version through Apple's TestFlight, Apple provides me with limited information such as crash reports and any feedback you choose to send, under Apple's terms.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
